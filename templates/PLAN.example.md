## Rules

- Down weeks as tabled, even when feeling great.
- Judge load on the athlete, not on a percentage. A big week-to-week jump is a prompt to check symptoms, easy-run HR at a fixed reference pace, sleep and how the quality sessions felt — not an automatic cut. (The familiar "~10% rule" is not evidence-based; see `notes/shared/training-load-rules.md`.) Catch *unplanned* accumulation hard; leave planned down-week rebounds alone.
- Fuelling is prescribed, not improvised: every long run carries a carbohydrate rate, escalating across the block, with race products locked well before race week.
- Missed ≤2 days: carry on, no make-up sessions. Missed 3+: re-plan the block.
- Never two quality days back to back.
- Strength sessions are part of the plan, not optional extras; taper them too.
- Paces recalibrate after the tune-up race.
